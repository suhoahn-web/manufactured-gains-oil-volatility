## A. The evaluation scale decides the ranking

The published evaluation uses neither: QLIKE is applied to volatility and squared error to log volatility. Both scales are reported here beside the one the result covers.

### Giacomini-Rossi fluctuation test

The loss differential is standardized over a rolling window and the largest absolute value compared with the critical value for the corresponding window fraction. Rejection means relative performance is not constant over the sample, which is a different and stronger statement than a split chosen after the fact.

## C. The benchmark is nested inside every rival

Each neural forecast is the fitted benchmark plus a learned correction, so under the null that the correction is worthless the rival equals the benchmark plus estimation noise. Diebold-Mariano is undersized in that case. Clark-West adjusts for it. The test is one-sided: a large positive statistic favours the larger model.
